# Retained multi-edition assessments

A multi-edition assessment keeps each retained original and its independently validated review. The outcome is a bounded documentary finding; it does not establish a current publisher edition, clinical acceptance or compliance. Preserve conflicting labels and unresolved incoming references in the review.

The multi-edition compiler requires every manifest source exactly once and checks the exact active relationship versions. The writer preserves the original completion and appends later revalidation evidence, using an exact assessment preimage and ETag. A caller must compare the complete relationship and catalogue snapshots, coordinate the graph writer through readback, and reconcile an uncertain response before any further send. Coordination is not a cross-object transaction.

Use the effective-evidence resolver when reading amendments: the root completion is historical and may not be the latest review. Supply exact archived bytes when prompted by its archive reference. Missing or unverifiable archives and unverified later history are reported explicitly rather than interpreted as no amendments. The available archive contract does not authenticate the current history head, so it cannot detect removal of a final unarchived entry or archive reference without separate trusted sequence evidence.

Large manifests may require the existing lossless history archive before a new amendment. Verify the archive bytes and native readback, compact conditionally, then reread and recompile the review against the new preimage. The archive retains the root and latest addendum, so it cannot guarantee that every oversized manifest can be compacted. Never overwrite the retained source, clinical decision or human edits.
