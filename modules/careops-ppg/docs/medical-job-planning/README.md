# CHHHS medical job planning flowcharts (draft)

Editable Mermaid flowcharts proposed for the medical job-planning procedure and guideline. **Not approved CHHHS instructions.**

- `01-end-to-end.mmd` — service requirements through consultation, compliance, approval and review.
- `02-disagreement-escalation.mmd` — immediate safety controls, industrial advice, resolution and escalation.
- `03-monitoring-review.mmd` — monitor, compare, consult, adjust and reapprove.

The accompanying offline working pack contains PNG/SVG **visual equivalents** generated from matching Graphviz node/edge topology, not Mermaid-engine render exports. Mermaid source is authoritative and may be rendered in a Mermaid-compatible viewer.
