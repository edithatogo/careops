# PPG remaining work — 2 November 2026

The core workbench, qualified collectors and datasets, document generation,
production reconciliation, reusable installer, dataset catalogue and bounded
accreditation examples have recorded delivery evidence. Historical partial
archives retain their incomplete criteria; this is not a new health check of
unchanged flows.

## Registry rollout

The only open production check is the first genuine update submitted by a
non-owner staff member from the correct Registry record. Before submission,
capture the earlier edition’s file ID, version and hash. After submission, open
the new edition through **Open linked files**, follow its own **Submitted for
review** case in the Review Queue, and confirm the earlier edition still opens
with the same ID, version and hash. Keep the evidence with the deployment.

Handle outstanding Registry links as files become available. For each
confirmed document and edition, link it and open it from the matching record.
Keep ambiguous candidates open in the existing private action list until the
source or custodian resolves them. This is ongoing catalogue maintenance.

Word review packs and new dashboards, including Power BI, are deferred until
staff identify a specific need in day-to-day use.

## Evidence and operational work

Policy currency matching, disposition reporting and its cloud schedule are
delivered. The latest CLI review examines full notices and policy bodies,
including individual medicine rows in monthly PI bulletins. Suggestions remain
candidates. Policy owners must decide applicability, current responsibility and
review disposition; unmatched never means no impact.

Remaining policy-body holds need a matching original or explicit publisher/
register correction, local adoption or supersession evidence. Missing notice
bodies, publisher verification challenges, cover messages without the issue and
misattached notices remain incomplete. Current approval and operative editions
are not inferred from retained bodies. Do not turn an identity conflict into an
alias just to improve coverage.

External source blockers remain METEOR access/signing and an allowed connector
route, Ahpra exchange agreement/interface/access, AusUDID automated release and
incremental semantics, missing genuine newsletter samples/senders, unselected
National Boards and inaccessible structured internal sources. The PMAQ publisher
approval conflict remains explicit; bounded fictional portability is delivered.

The archived broader relationship-screening and external-publication catalogue
tracks remain unfinished. They are not prerequisites for the delivered bounded
workbench and are not represented as completed plans.
