- PC-01/PC-02 delivered: the SharePoint review view links source leads to candidate
  policy identities, recorded custodians and supporting evidence; repeat refresh
  preserves human dispositions.
- PC-03 delivered historically: a local new-lead job and disposition reporting.
  The local job was retired after PC-04 cloud commissioning.
- PC-04 delivered: solution-owned cloud matching and disposition coverage.
- PC-05 delivered: bounded source-evidence review and preserved uncertain matches.
- PC-06a delivered: two-sided full-notice/full-policy review with explicit capture,
  extraction and edition provenance. PC-06b full notice coverage remains incomplete.
- PC-07a delivered: body-identity qualifier, additive catalogue ledger and grouped
  review view. PC-07b retains unresolved original/adoption/identifier/scope evidence
  holds. A retained edition is not proof of current approval.
- PC-08 delivered: hash-bound PI table extraction for explicit row-level review.
  Preserve ingredient, brand/sponsor, changes and raw approval date. Preparation
  does not establish applicability. Companion 1.4.1 versions the extractor; cloud
  solution definitions and schedules remain unchanged.

Installation-specific counts, selected policy links, readback/replay results and
operational receipts remain in private evidence. Existing candidates, human
input and source-register identities are preserved. Bounded search does not prove
source absence. PC-06b/PC-07b remain incomplete; none of these tools makes clinical
applicability or disposition decisions. See [remaining PPG plans](../../../docs/ppg-remaining-work.md).
