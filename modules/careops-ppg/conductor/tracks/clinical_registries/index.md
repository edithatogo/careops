# Clinical registries register

## Purpose and integration

Provide an organisation-wide register of locally operated clinical registries and participation in external registries. Keep Registry identity separate from Participation episodes, including service/site, period, accountable roles and provenance. Deployment-specific scope, bindings and evidence remain private.

Reuse PPG's external publication/edition/capture catalogue, source-qualified requirements, review contexts, evidence versions, assessments and mappings. Resolve registry/participation scope through review contexts; do not manufacture a document identity for a registry. Existing research records remain with the research module; meeting decisions and actions remain with Clinical Hub. Use stable references rather than duplicate records.

Public reference: Australian Commission on Safety and Quality in Health Care, *Australian Framework for National Clinical Quality Registries 2024*, August 2024. Retain source editions, capture hashes and page locators privately. Distinguish voluntary framework expectations from locally determined applicability, mandatory requirements and clinical assurance. The code licence grants no redistribution rights over external sources.

## Native qualification boundary

Catalogue persistence must preserve the existing owner, exact target/account pins, unique stable keys, complete payload/hash validation, serial intent journal, one create attempt and exact-key/field readback. Reconcile uncertain outcomes by reading; never blindly retry a mutation. Establish current target-specific notification effects before writing. Catalogue creation does not qualify capture upload, formatter/view changes, schema changes, flow activation or message delivery. Preserve existing holds for unqualified operations.

Technical checks do not establish clinical compliance, complete real-registry coverage, production adoption or operational acceptance. No native standard registration or register implementation is claimed by this source reference. Subsequent source and native work stays with its existing integrators and object owners.
