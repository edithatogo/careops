# Specification

Status: retained roadmap requirements. The current implementation is a deterministic synthetic capacity calculator; operational evaluation and advanced analyses remain unqualified.

## Goal

Provide a reproducible model of current and target credentialing workload,
costs, quality, benefits, and capacity release across the modular MVP lifecycle.

## Requirements

- activity-based stages, roles, volumes, touches, durations, rework, and rates;
- one-off and recurrent cost separation;
- capacity release distinguished from cash savings;
- named destination for released staff time;
- quality, safety, user, privacy, accessibility, and workload balancing measures;
- current, synthetic, retrospective silent, prospective silent, integrated, and
  steady-state comparators;
- scenario and sensitivity analysis;
- three- to five-year budget impact;
- distributional analysis by pathway, profession, service, facility, and rural or
  remote setting;
- AI costs, review burden, errors, fallback, and benefits represented explicitly;
- benefits register with owner, baseline, target, method, source, attribution,
  confidence, frequency, and realisation status.

## Acceptance criteria

- no headcount or cash-saving assumption from time release alone;
- no double counting across time, avoided cost, cash cost, and opportunity
  value;
- current-state data and silent-pilot effects remain visibly separate;
- uncertainty and attribution are explicit;
- quality and unintended burden can offset time savings;
- reusable option value is reported separately from realised benefit; and
- source data and operational outputs remain outside Git.
