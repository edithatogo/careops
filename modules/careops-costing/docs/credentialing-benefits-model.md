# Credentialing workload, capacity-release, and benefits model

Status: model specification; no operational baseline data loaded

## Purpose

This model evaluates whether a CareOps credentialing capability improves
quality and assurance while releasing staff time from repetitive administration
for complex review, practitioner support, professional judgement, and service
improvement.

It is not a headcount-reduction model. Time released is a benefit only when the
service identifies what work should reduce, what higher-value work should
increase, and whether safety, quality, timeliness, staff experience, and
practitioner experience improve.

## Comparator

Compare at least:

1. current process baseline;
2. target process in synthetic or retrospective silent mode;
3. prospective silent mode;
4. limited integrated pilot; and
5. steady state after implementation and learning.

Separate one-off discovery, design, migration, training, change, and
implementation costs from recurrent licence, support, governance, data,
security, model, and operating costs.

## Activity-based workload model

For each pathway and stage record:

- case volume;
- staff role and cost rate;
- number and duration of manual touches;
- waiting time and active work time;
- repeat requests and rework;
- committee and professional-review preparation;
- exceptions and escalation;
- quality assurance, audit, reporting, and reconciliation;
- training, support, and maintenance;
- automation or AI review time; and
- residual human judgement.

Primary stages are intake, validation, correction, verification, professional
and service review, committee preparation, committee consideration, delegated
decision, communication, register activation, expiry, renewal, conditions,
supervision, review, and appeal.

## Cost categories

### One-off

- current-state discovery and data profiling;
- policy and authority reconciliation;
- product, workflow, data, security, records, accessibility, and integration
  design;
- Power Platform and repository implementation;
- source extraction, cleansing, mapping, migration rehearsals, and cutover;
- testing, privacy, security, legal, clinical, and audit assurance;
- training, change, communications, backfill, and transition support;
- legacy archival and decommissioning.

### Recurrent

- Power Platform, Microsoft 365, Power BI/Fabric, AI, OCR, storage, and connector
  licences or consumption;
- platform administration and environment management;
- workflow, rule, prompt, model, policy, and taxonomy maintenance;
- monitoring, support, incidents, continuity, backup, and recovery;
- data quality, reconciliation, reporting, audit, and access review;
- committee and professional time;
- manual exception handling and practitioner support.

## Benefits

### Measured administrative benefits

- fewer manual touches and duplicate entries;
- lower correction and repeat-evidence demand;
- shorter active processing and elapsed time;
- reduced paper assembly and correspondence preparation;
- lower register and reporting reconciliation effort;
- reduced retrospective audit-pack preparation;
- fewer expired, unreconciled, duplicate, or orphaned cases;
- reduced unsupported legacy-system maintenance.

### Quality and assurance benefits

- improved complete-at-first-submission rate;
- more consistent pathway and evidence rules;
- current authority and delegation checks;
- clearer recommendation, decision, condition, supervision, expiry, review,
  appeal, and release records;
- improved procedural-fairness evidence;
- stronger conformance, exception, and audit visibility;
- earlier identification of workload, bottleneck, and capacity risks.

### Capacity-release benefits

Record the destination of released time, such as:

- complex case and exception review;
- proactive practitioner support;
- professional and service-capability assurance;
- temporary, conditional, and supervision monitoring;
- policy and taxonomy maintenance;
- data quality and reconciliation;
- committee quality and decision support;
- service improvement, education, research, and workforce planning.

Do not count released time twice or assume it is cash-releasing. Report time,
capacity, avoided cost, cash cost, and opportunity value separately.

## Outcomes and balancing measures

- turnaround by stage and pathway;
- first-pass completeness and rework;
- false alerts and missed requirements;
- staff, practitioner, committee, and reviewer experience;
- staff overtime, backlog, leave, turnover, and vacancy pressure where relevant;
- number and age of exceptions;
- quality and audit findings;
- privacy, security, records, accessibility, and incident outcomes;
- decision and register accuracy;
- delayed commencement or service disruption;
- cost per completed case;
- total cost of ownership;
- capacity redirected to named higher-value work.

## Economic analysis

Use:

- activity-based costing for current and target workflows;
- budget impact over at least three to five years;
- cost-consequence analysis with benefits reported separately;
- scenario and probabilistic sensitivity analysis for volumes, time saved,
  licence use, implementation duration, adoption, error rates, and support;
- break-even and payback analysis;
- distributional analysis by profession, facility, rural/remote location,
  pathway, and user group; and
- option value for reuse across PPG assurance, committees, approvals, and other
  CareOps modules.

Avoid a single-point ROI claim until current-state data and silent-pilot effects
are observed. Maintain a benefits register with owner, baseline, target,
measurement method, evidence source, attribution, confidence, frequency, and
realisation status.

## AI-specific costs and benefits

Measure OCR, model, prompt, agent, review, storage, telemetry, and assurance
costs. Benefits may include faster extraction, reconciliation, summarisation,
correspondence drafting, and test generation. Subtract human review, correction,
false-positive, false-negative, privacy, monitoring, retraining, and fallback
costs.

AI is justified only where it adds net value over deterministic automation and
human work while preserving quality and the normal non-AI pathway.
