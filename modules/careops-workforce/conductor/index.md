# CareOps Workforce — Conductor

CareOps Workforce owns the operational medical job-planning application. Its existing `Workforce.pbip` is a separate source-locked reporting asset. Neither the dashboard nor the credentialing capability is the application database.

- [CHHHS medical job planning Power App track](tracks/chhhs_medical_job_planning_powerapp_20261111/index.md): MoSCoW requirements, design, contracts and executable JP-00–JP-06 development/qualification tasks; status **planned, not implemented**.
- [Workforce reporting project](../README.md): source-locked PBIR/TMDL and feed-bound release separate from app commissioning.

Optional bounded integrations: credentialing read-only effective-dated SoCP references, PPG document/template authority links, Approve/Decisions event contracts, aggregate Finance/Workforce reporting. No authoritative register, roster, payroll or staff case modifications by default.
