# CHHHS read-only current-system discovery

Status: next organisational gate

The repository checklist at `docs/credentialing/chhhs-read-only-discovery-checklist.md` defines the participants, minimum field set, adapter order, silent-evaluation sequence, measures and stop conditions for the first approved current-system discovery.

The discovery should be short and practical: one routine-renewal pathway, one Cairns Services walkthrough, one Rural and Remote Services walkthrough, and one minimum-necessary read-only data route. It should identify existing strengths and workarounds before proposing changes.

Outputs are:

1. current-state process and decision-rights map;
2. authoritative-source and record map;
3. supported interface assessment;
4. minimum field and privacy classification;
5. exception and failure-path map;
6. baseline workload, quality and burden measures;
7. read-only silent-evaluation plan; and
8. a clear decision on whether to proceed, modify or stop.

No live access, extraction or comparison begins until the business owner, system owner, Records Management, privacy, cyber security and Clinical Governance requirements are confirmed.
