# Specification — Publication and citation time-series products

## Goal
Compute annual publications by date precision, type, OA, journal, specialty/topic, author, collaborations and fractionally counted output; longitudinal citation snapshots and age-normalised rates where defensible.

## Requirements

**Must:** Versioned denominators, publication cohorts and extraction timestamps; source-qualified citation coverage and citation vintages; exclude naive counts summed across providers; missingness and uncertainty visible.

**Should:** retain replayable provenance, preserve data rights, integrate with existing RIMS contracts where equivalent and document divergences.

**Could:** add additional provider-supported metrics only if independently evaluated and source licensing permits.

**Won't:** assert live collection, RIMS native deployment, private employment affiliation or unverified CHHHS attribution.

## Acceptance criteria
Reproduce fixture metrics with independent calculations; source-vintage drift, incomplete years, lag and historical backfill tests; distinguish descriptive indicators from causal impact.

## Evidence
No implementation or hosted test evidence recorded at creation. Record exact tests, commits, source snapshots and reviewer decisions in a subsequent evidence file.
