# N04 specification

Use service-owned SharePoint libraries for approved evidence, instruments and
correspondence, with stable version references in Dataverse. Avoid personal
OneDrive, duplicate submissions and an editable Lists register mirror.

Owner: Records/information owner with privacy and platform leads. Backup and
records retention are different. Dataverse access does not automatically prove
SharePoint access. Acceptance: every task in plan.md, including direct-link
security and cross-service restoration reconciliation.
