## Required native and operational follow-through

The all-update/no-filter post-operation choice is deliberate: compare the actual
row result, not merely a mutable incoming patch. Keep the four minimal image
columns and measure real cost. Native UpdateMultiple/Upsert/specialized and nested
paths, actual images, rollback, missing registrations and bypass privileges all
require approved sandbox tests. Source mocks do not prove native behavior.

Disabled registration metadata is not functioning protection. A controlled installer
must verify assembly and metadata provenance, collisions, state/image behavior,
solution membership and approved rank. Enablement requires its separate acceptance;
production cannot proceed with a required guard left disabled. Requested
CanBeBypassed=false does not establish administrator-proof enforcement.

Creation integrity, authoritative human/case/source/delegation binding, decision/
event immutability, deletion/retention and an auditable identity-correction process
remain distinct open tasks. Continue native read/write/permission/rollback/recovery
and workbench acceptance, rather than closing N03 from this invariant alone.
Current signed CHHHS instruments are not certified by these tests.
