# N08 plan: human governance and pathway completeness

Owner: Credentialing governance owner, to be named. All tasks planned.

- [ ] N08.01 Agree a complete local pathway/profession/service inventory and ownership; accept explicit included, deferred and continuing-legacy categories.
- [ ] N08.02 Implement committee membership, relevant peer, conflicts, quorum and records; accept conflict/quorum failure and authorised alternative pathway tests.
- [ ] N08.03 Implement initial application and renewal as distinct rules; accept local duration/evidence/calendar tests and reuse of valid existing material.
- [ ] N08.04 Implement changed scope/new-service capability checks; accept separation of craft model endorsement, service approval and individual approval.
- [ ] N08.05 Implement mutual recognition and statewide/regional handling; accept primary-approval/expiry/conditions and current schedule checks without repeat practitioner work.
- [ ] N08.06 Implement separately approved interim/urgent/emergency/disaster subtypes; accept distinct authorities, hard expiry, non-renewal and subsequent review where required.
- [ ] N08.07 Implement conditions, supervision and follow-up ownership; accept visible operational limitations and no automatic clearance of outstanding requirements.
- [ ] N08.08 Implement review/adverse-decision/appeal paths; accept notices, response opportunities, recusals, reasons and approved communications before effects.
- [ ] N08.09 Record recommendation and delegated decision separately, then controlled register reconciliation; accept no committee recommendation or Teams click alone activating scope.
- [ ] N08.10 Replay accepted cases across local professional streams and Cairns/rural services; accept clinical/legal governance review and each unsupported route explicitly retained outside the release.

This track adapts workflow to approved instruments; it does not itself approve
new clinical models, delegates, facilities, scope or practitioner outcomes.
