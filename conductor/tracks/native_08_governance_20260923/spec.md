# N08 specification

Implement pathway-specific human recommendation, delegation, procedural fairness
and decision recording with no universal committee-first rule where approved
interim pathways differ. Preserve currently authorised local practice.

Owner: credentialing governance owner. Distinct profession schedules and service
capability overlays apply; no blanket medical workflow imposed on other streams.
Acceptance: each in-scope pathway has explicit positive/adverse/exception cases
and source/authority evidence, not only a routine-renewal demonstration.
