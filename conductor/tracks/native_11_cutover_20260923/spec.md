# N11 specification

Replace the current product only after supported export, reconciliation,
representative acceptance and explicit cutover authority. Preserve existing
approval dates, limitations, reviews and source evidence; migration grants no scope.

Owner: business/migration owner with records, Digital Health and credentialing.
No assumption about contract period, automatic savings or immediate cancellation.
Acceptance: complete in-scope population/pathway reconciliation, safe downtime
and rollback, native qualification and accountable release evidence.
