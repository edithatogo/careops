# N10 specification

Qualify the whole native service against approved security, clinical, privacy,
capacity, recovery and continuity requirements. Unit coverage and successful
solution packing are necessary evidence, not operating-effectiveness proof.

Owner: service assurance owner with independent cyber/clinical reviewers. Audit
and record retention, backup, service recovery and downtime practice are separate
controls. Acceptance requires actual drills and recorded residual risk; no
invented vendor SLA or untested same-tenant backup independence.
