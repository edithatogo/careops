# N07 specification

Provide a simple model-driven Power Apps workbench and searchable role-scoped
register. Tailor views for clinical/professional, credentialing, administrative,
workforce and practitioner users without relying on profession as authorisation.

Owner: product/accessibility owner. Modules retain their standalone screens; an
integrated shell is optional. Colours supplement text, evidence and next action.
Acceptance: plan.md tasks, genuine task-based usability and direct-access security;
no added form, duplicate approval or separate agent-review task.
