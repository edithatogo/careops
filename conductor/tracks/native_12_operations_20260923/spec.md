# N12 specification

Establish sustained operating effectiveness, support ownership and future
module/platform exit. Production deployment is not completion; evidence must
cover an agreed observation period and scheduled reassessment.

Owner: named service owner for each enabled module. Apply proportional maturity
criteria to standalone purposes; do not keep a migration service running without
need. Acceptance: plan.md results, realised benefits and safe records-preserving
retirement. Recurring controls remain ongoing even when initial delivery closes.
