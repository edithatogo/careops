# N12 plan: supported operation through retirement

Owner: Production service owner, to be named. All tasks planned.

- [ ] N12.01 Establish hypercare and funded steady-state support/on-call coverage; accept named owners, escalation times and tested absence arrangements.
- [ ] N12.02 Observe agreed reliability, data quality and safety measures; accept measured service objectives and reviewed incidents rather than uptime assumptions.
- [ ] N12.03 Measure total workload/experience across users and support; accept net effort saved without shifted burden or increased missed requirements.
- [ ] N12.04 Validate full lifecycle costs and realised benefits; accept licence/capacity/AI/support/backup/migration/overlap costs against the verified contract baseline.
- [ ] N12.05 Schedule access, delegation, policy-source and configuration recertification; accept accountable changes without autonomous policy promotion.
- [ ] N12.06 Rehearse recovery and downtime at an approved cadence and after material change; accept current drill evidence and reconciled deficiencies.
- [ ] N12.07 Requalify upgrades, dependencies and optional agents against native/standalone tests; accept bounded rollout and rollback, not self-improving production edits.
- [ ] N12.08 Complete module-specific M0-M7 records and service acceptance; accept independent evidence per enabled module, not a portfolio-wide completion claim.
- [ ] N12.09 Rehearse export and future module/platform retirement preserving records and legal holds; accept no dependency cascade or data loss from uninstall.
- [ ] N12.10 Close initial programme only after operating/legacy-exit gates; accept signed handover, open risk ownership and ongoing review dates with no claim of permanent completion.

Recurring maintenance tasks close their initial setup only; subsequent reviews
and drills remain owned operational obligations with separately dated evidence.
