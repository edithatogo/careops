# Product definition

CareOps is a source control plane for a portfolio of low-authority operational workflow components
that use existing Microsoft 365 and Power Platform permissions. The portfolio
coordinates independently deployable modules, maintained directly here where migrated or in catalogued repositories while keeping confidential
tenant and operational data outside source control.

The parent repository is PRIVATE under the current user direction. Reviewed
reusable source remains distinct from private evidence and does not grant public
publication authority. Independent child-repository visibility and protection
are qualified separately through the [privacy continuation](tracks/suite_ci_delivery_20261018/privacy-rollout-20261103.md).
